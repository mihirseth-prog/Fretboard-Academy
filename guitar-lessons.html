The roadmap

A structure that actually works

Most people fail at guitar because they jump around with no plan. Here's the sequence that gets results.

1

Get comfortable with the instrument

Posture, tuning, parts of the guitar, and holding picks. Takes one session.

2

Learn your first open chords

Four chords, clean chord changes, and your first complete song.

3

Build rhythm and strumming

Count beats, lock into a groove, and make every chord change feel musical.

4

Expand your chord vocabulary

Minor chords, 7ths, and eventually barre chords to play in any key.

5

Learn the fretboard & start soloing

Scales, positions, and how to improvise melodies that sound intentional.
