    The story

    Teaching guitar is my life's work

    I started playing at 14, teaching at 19, and haven't stopped since. Fretboard Academy was built because I saw too many beginners quit — not because guitar is hard, but because the way it's taught usually is.

    Every lesson here is designed around one question: what does someone actually need to know right now to sound better? Not what's traditionally taught first. What works.

    Teaching isn't about knowing more.
    It's about explaining better.

    Over 20 years of teaching, 12,000+ students, and one simple belief: anyone can learn to play if they're shown the right path in the right order.

    — Jamie Cole

    Common questions

    Do I need to own a guitar to start? ▼
    You'll need a guitar to practice, but you can watch the first few lessons first to decide which type to buy. We have a free buying guide in the beginner section.
    What if I'm a complete beginner? ▼
    Perfect — that's who we design for first. Our beginner path assumes zero prior knowledge and gets you playing songs within the first week.
    Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
    Yes. If you follow the 30-day beginner plan and don't play your first complete song, we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
    Can I switch plans later? ▼
    Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade any time. We'll prorate the difference if you're mid-cycle.
    Do lessons work on mobile? ▼
    Yes — everything is fully responsive. Many students watch on their phone or tablet while their guitar is in their hands.
