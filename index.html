The story

Teaching guitar is our life's work

We both picked up the guitar at age 9 and never put it down. Fretboard Academy was built because we saw too many beginners quit — not because guitar is hard, but because the way it's taught usually is.

Every lesson here is designed around one question: what does someone actually need to know right now to sound better? Not what's traditionally taught first. What works. We've only taught a handful of students so far, but we're committed to helping as many people as possible learn guitar.

Teaching isn't about knowing more.
It's about explaining better.

Two lifelong guitarists, playing since age 9, with one simple belief: anyone can learn to play if they're shown the right path in the right order.

— Mihir & Ranvir

Common questions

Do I need to own a guitar to start? ▼
You'll need a guitar to practice, but you can watch the first free lesson first to decide which type to buy.
What if I'm a complete beginner? ▼
Perfect — that's who we design for first. Our beginner path assumes zero prior knowledge and gets you playing songs within the first week.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
Yes. If you complete 3 lessons and don't enjoy it, we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
Can I switch plans later? ▼
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade any time. We'll prorate the difference if you're mid-cycle.
Do lessons work on mobile? ▼
Yes — everything is fully responsive. Many students watch on their phone or tablet while their guitar is in their hands.
