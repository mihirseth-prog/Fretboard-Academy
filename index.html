The story

Teaching guitar is our life's work

We're both 16, and we've been playing guitar since age 9. Fretboard Academy was built because we saw too many beginners quit — not because guitar is hard, but because the way it's taught usually is.

Every lesson here is designed around one question: what does someone actually need to know right now to sound better? Not what's traditionally taught first. What works. Based in Long Grove, Illinois, we've only taught a handful of students so far, but we're committed to helping as many people as possible learn guitar — and we bring a fresh, modern perspective to teaching that older instructors sometimes miss.

Teaching isn't about knowing more.
It's about explaining better.

Two 16-year-old guitarists, playing since age 9, with one simple belief: anyone can learn to play if they're shown the right path in the right order.

— Mihir & Ranvir

Common questions

Do I need to own a guitar to start? ▼
You'll need a guitar to practice, but you can watch the first free lesson first to decide which type to buy.
What if I'm a complete beginner? ▼
Perfect — that's who we design for first. Our beginner path assumes zero prior knowledge and gets you playing songs within the first week.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
Yes. If you complete 3 lessons and don't enjoy it, we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
Can I switch plans later? ▼
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade any time.
Do lessons work on mobile? ▼
Yes — everything is fully responsive. Many students watch on their phone or tablet while their guitar is in their hands.
